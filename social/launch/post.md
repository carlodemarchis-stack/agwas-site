A Guy with a Scarf has a home: aguywithascarf.com

The name came from a stage at Leaders Week in London, when David Cushnan introduced me as a guy with a scarf. The newsletter followed on 15 November 2022. Four years later there is a weekly newsletter with 5,101 subscribers on LinkedIn (as of 4 November), 61 recorded conversations, a research hub, a twelve-lesson course with a 200-page book, and ten live sport data experiences built from official feeds and updated every night. Until last week they were scattered across platforms.

What is on the page:

1. Who I work with and on what, in one sentence with two menus: executive teams on streaming economics, founders on product and platform strategy, boards on retention and monetisation.
2. Thirty-five years on the vendor side, Deltatre from 1988 to 2023, and what I do with them now: advisory, fractional roles, moderation, keynotes, content partnerships, the course, the data work.
3. The stage record, eight stages in twelve months, with dates and roles.
4. Every channel with its numbers. For getting in touch, a LinkedIn message reaches me the same day.

There is also a mark. Four bars in the colours of the scarf, solid on the left, breaking into particles on the right: a broadcast becoming data. That edge, what we know on the left and what is still forming on the right, is where I have worked since the first web team at Deltatre in 1995. The scarf is an accident that stuck.

One brand from here on, A Guy with a Scarf.
