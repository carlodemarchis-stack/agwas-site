About

LinkedIn ↗

I work at the intersection of sports, media and technology, helping leaders through the moments where strategy, product and business model collide.

Most of my professional life has been spent on the vendor side, long enough to have seen several cycles repeat themselves with different labels and similar outcomes. The view is pragmatic: innovation grounded in execution, not theory.

I advise companies in this industry, Dolby OptiView and Mercury Intermedia among them, so read the writing as interested rather than neutral. The data experiences I build myself, in the evenings. The scarf is real.
